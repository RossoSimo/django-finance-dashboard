How it works

Upload a screenshot of your bank, broker or portfolio app, or of a list of your investments. Each position and cash balance is read, then matched to a real market listing (by ticker, ISIN or name) and checked against today's price.

  • Best results: a sharp, cropped screenshot of the positions table, with quantity and price or value visible.
  • Reading takes about 20–40 seconds (longer the first time, while the model loads).
